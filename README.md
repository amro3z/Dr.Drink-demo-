# Description
The "Drink Daily App" is designed to help users develop and maintain the healthy habit of drinking enough water every day. It aims to promote better hydration through features such as:

# Water Intake Reminders

The app reminds users to drink water at regular intervals throughout the day to ensure they stay hydrated.
# Tracking and Logging

Users can log their water intake, and the app tracks daily, weekly, and monthly progress, providing clear and organized records.
# Personalized Recommendations

The app provides recommendations on how much water to drink based on factors like
Weather conditions: Adapts hydration goals based on temperature and humidity.
User information: Gender, weight, and activity levels.
#Insights and Tips

It delivers insights and health tips to help users understand the importance of hydration and its effects on energy levels, skin health, and overall well-being.
# Engaging Interface

The app uses a user-friendly design with motivational messages and visual progress indicators to encourage users to stay consistent.
